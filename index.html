Your details, handled seriously

The party is a joke. Your data is not.

  • What we collect: your name, work email and (optionally) team — nothing else. No passwords, phone numbers or employee IDs.
  • Why: to keep the MCK member list and to share fun team-games campaign updates.
  • Where it lives: a private spreadsheet that only the organiser can open. The member list is never published on this site.
  • What we don’t do: we don’t record or infer how anyone votes, and we don’t share member details with other nominees or third parties.
  • Change your mind? Ask the organiser to correct or delete your registration: contact details coming soon.
