The MCK Manifesto

Keep the match winners on the field. Let us take care of the games!

  1. 01

    More games, less boredom.

    Boredom will be politely but firmly escorted out of the building.

  2. 02

    More team bonding, fewer excuses.

    “I have a call” remains valid. We will simply schedule around it.

  3. 03

    Match winners stay on the field.

    Our star players play. The committee handles the clipboards.

  4. 04

    Great ideas deserve great execution.

    Every suggestion gets a fair hearing — and an actual plan.

*Playful campaign promises. Legally non-binding. Powered entirely by enthusiasm. Not company-approved initiatives.

Your details, handled seriously

The party is a joke. Your data is not.

What we collect
Your name, work email and (optionally) team — nothing else. No passwords, phone numbers or employee IDs.
Why
To keep the MCK member list and to share fun team-games campaign updates.
Where it lives
A private spreadsheet that only the organiser can open. The member list is never published on this site.
What we don’t do
We don’t record or infer how anyone votes, and we don’t share member details with other nominees or third parties.
